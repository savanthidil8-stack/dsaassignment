FINAL CONCLUSION
================

Both Merge Sort and Quick Sort successfully sort
the package weights in ascending order.

Merge Sort preserves the original relative order
of equal-weight packages.

Therefore, Merge Sort is more suitable when
maintaining the original order of equal-weight
packages is important.
